# Brief: your 401(k) match

**From:** People Team, Alder Street Financial (fictional)
**For:** new hires in their first week, most of them in their twenties, watching on a phone or laptop
**Length:** 60–75 seconds, 16:9 (a vertical cut is a bonus)
**After watching they should:** raise their contribution to at least 5% in the benefits portal.

## Source: Alder Street Retirement Plan summary, 2027 plan year (fictional)

- You are enrolled automatically at **3%** of pay on your first day. Each January it goes up by **1 point**
  until it reaches **10%**, unless you change it.
- Alder Street matches **100% of the first 3%** you contribute and **50% of the next 2%**. Contribute 5%
  and the company adds **4%**. Contribute more than 5% and the match stays at 4%.
- The match vests over three years: **33%** after year one, **66%** after year two, **100%** after year
  three. Your own contributions are always 100% yours.
- Example in the summary: a salary of **$60,000**. At 3%, you put in $1,800 and the company adds $1,800.
  At 5%, you put in $3,000 and the company adds **$2,400**. The difference in the match is **$600 a year**.
- Change your rate any time: Benefits portal → Retirement → Contribution rate. It applies from the next
  paycheck.

## Tone

Warm, plain, a little playful. No jargon without a picture. Not a lecture about saving.
