# Judging a bench round

A fresh reviewer who has not seen the storyboards judges the films from their pictures, the way a
viewer would. Scores are 1–10; write one sentence of evidence for each.

Inputs per film: the brief (`bench/enterprise/*.md`), the encoded film's one-frame-per-second
timeline (`build/qa/timeline.png`, or frames extracted from the MP4), the 360 px phone sheet
(`build/qa/phone.png`) and the `qa` summary (change per second, seconds held under the voice).

1. **Not a slideshow.** Does the picture carry the explanation and keep changing with the voice,
   with something carried across cuts? (1 = titled cards with a sentence each; 10 = a film.)
2. **Clear.** Would the audience in the brief know what to do afterwards, from one viewing?
3. **Readable.** At phone size: type large enough, on screen long enough, never fighting the picture.
4. **Craft.** Composition, type, colour, spacing; nothing cut off, overlapping or empty.
5. **Delight.** An idea, a metaphor, a moment of wit or surprise beyond literal illustration.
6. **Fit.** Right tone for a company or financial firm: trustworthy, warm, not gimmicky, no disclaimers.
7. **Distinct.** Would you confuse it with the other films in the round?

Also name the single best and the single worst moment (with the time), and the three changes that
would help most. Report a table of scores and the mean of the seven.
