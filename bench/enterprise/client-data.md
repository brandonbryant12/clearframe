# Brief: client data stays in the building

**From:** Risk and Compliance, Meridian Wealth Partners (fictional)
**For:** advisers and assistants (420 people) who email and share files with clients every day
**Length:** 45–60 seconds, 16:9
**After watching they should:** share client documents only through **SecureShare**, starting **June 1**.

## Source: Policy RC-31, client information handling (fictional), effective June 1, 2028

- From **June 1**, client statements, tax forms and ID copies go to clients **only** through SecureShare,
  never as email attachments.
- Last year **27** misdirected emails sent client documents to the wrong person (internal incident log,
  2027). Each one had to be reported to the regulator.
- SecureShare links expire after **7 days**, and the client signs in to open them.
- In Outlook: write the email as usual, then press **Share securely** instead of Attach. The file goes up,
  the client gets a link.
- Personal email, USB drives and consumer file-sharing apps are not allowed for client documents.

## Tone

Matter-of-fact, respectful of busy people. Not a scolding. Show that it is the same email with one
different button.
