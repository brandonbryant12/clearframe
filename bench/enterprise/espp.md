# Brief: the share plan opens again

**From:** Reward team, Alder Street Group (fictional, 9,800 employees in three countries)
**For:** all employees; most have never joined the share plan and think it is "for managers"
**Length:** about 60 seconds, 16:9
**After watching they should:** decide whether to join the Share Purchase Plan before **May 31**.

## Source: Alder Street Share Purchase Plan, 2028 offering (fictional)

- Twice a year you can buy Alder Street shares at a **15% discount** from your pay.
- You choose how much: **1% to 10%** of base pay, taken each payday for six months.
- The price you pay is 15% below the **lower** of two prices: the share price on the first day of the six
  months, or on the last day. (Example in the plan guide: start price $40, end price $50: you pay $34.)
- There is no lock-up: you can sell the shares as soon as they are bought.
- Leave the plan any time before the purchase date and your money comes back with your next pay.
- Join in the Reward hub → Share plan → Join, before **May 31**. Questions: reward@alder.example.

## Tone

Plain and fair. Not a sales pitch, no promises about the share price. The surprising part is "the lower of
two prices".
