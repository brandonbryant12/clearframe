# Brief: the new expense policy in one minute

**From:** Finance Operations, Fairhaven Private Wealth (fictional)
**For:** staff who travel or entertain clients; they skim policy PDFs and get expenses rejected
**Length:** 45–60 seconds, 16:9
**After watching they should:** submit within 30 days, with an itemised receipt, using the app.

## Source: Fairhaven Travel & Expense Policy v4, effective January 1, 2028 (fictional)

- Submit within **30 days** of the expense (was 60). Late claims need a director's approval.
- **Itemised receipts** for anything over **$25**. A card slip is not a receipt.
- Meals with clients: up to **$90 per person**, names and the client's firm on the claim.
- Per diem when travelling: **$75 a day** in most cities, **$95** in the 12 high-cost cities listed in
  appendix B. No receipts needed for per diem.
- Use the **Fairhaven Expenses app**: photograph the receipt, it fills the amount and date. Most claims are
  paid in **5 business days**.
- 2027 data: **31%** of claims were returned for a missing or non-itemised receipt.

## Tone

Light and quick; finance people making fun of their own policy a little. Never preachy.
