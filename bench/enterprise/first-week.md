# Brief: your first week

**From:** People team, Harbor Federal Credit Union (fictional, 1,150 staff)
**For:** new hires, shown on their first morning; many are branch staff in their first office job
**Length:** about 60 seconds, 16:9 (also cut vertical for the onboarding app)
**After watching they should:** finish the three first-week tasks by **Friday**.

## Source: Harbor Federal new-hire checklist, 2028 (fictional)

- Day 1: collect your badge at reception, and sign in to **Harbor Hub** with the temporary password in
  your welcome email. Change it the first time you sign in.
- By Friday, three tasks in Harbor Hub → My first week:
  1. **Bank details** for your pay (your first pay is on the **15th**; miss Friday and it is the 30th).
  2. **Benefits**: you have **30 days** from your start date to choose; if you don't, you get the basic
     plan only.
  3. **Security training**: 20 minutes, required before you can serve members.
- Your buddy is the person who met you at reception. Ask them anything.

## Tone

Warm, a welcome, a little wit. They are nervous and full of forms; make it feel like three small things.
