# Brief: we will never ask for your code

**From:** Fraud Prevention, Harbor Federal Bank (fictional)
**For:** retail banking customers on Instagram and in the app; many are over 60
**Length:** 30–40 seconds, 9:16 vertical
**After watching they should:** never share a one-time code, and hang up and call the number on their card.

## Source: Harbor Federal fraud bulletin, Q4 2027 (fictional)

- Scammers call pretending to be the bank and ask for the **6-digit code** we text you. With it they can
  move your money.
- Harbor Federal will **never** call, text or email to ask for that code.
- In Q4, **1 in 3** fraud reports from our customers started with a call like this.
- If anyone asks for your code: **hang up** and call the number on the back of your card.
- Report it in the app: Help → Report fraud.

## Tone

Calm, direct, protective. No scare music, no hooded hackers.
