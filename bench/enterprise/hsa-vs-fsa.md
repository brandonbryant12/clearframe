# Brief: HSA or FSA?

**From:** Benefits, Kestrel Mutual (fictional)
**For:** employees choosing a plan during open enrollment
**Length:** 60–80 seconds, 16:9
**After watching they should:** know which account goes with which plan, and which one is theirs to keep.

## Source: Kestrel Mutual 2027 Benefits Guide, "Spending accounts" (fictional plan terms)

- **HSA (health savings account)**: only with the Kestrel Saver high-deductible plan. Kestrel adds $750
  single / $1,500 family. **Yours to keep**, even if you leave Kestrel. Unused money **rolls over every
  year** and can be invested once the balance passes $1,000. Pre-tax in, tax-free growth, tax-free out
  for medical costs.
- **FSA (flexible spending account)**: with any Kestrel plan. You choose an amount up to **$3,300**. Pre-tax.
  **Use it or lose it**: up to $660 carries over into the next year; the rest is forfeited. Stays with
  Kestrel if you leave.
- Both pay for the same kinds of costs: prescriptions, co-pays, dental and vision work.
- You can't have both a full HSA and a full FSA in the same year (a limited-purpose FSA for dental and
  vision is allowed with the HSA).

## Tone

Friendly, concrete, with an everyday example rather than tax vocabulary.
