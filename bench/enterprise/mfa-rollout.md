# Brief: two-step sign-in is coming

**From:** IT, Kestrel Mutual (fictional)
**For:** all 4,200 employees, including branch staff who rarely read IT email
**Length:** 45–60 seconds, 16:9
**After watching they should:** set up the Kestrel Verify app before **March 3**.

## Source: IT change notice CN-2028-014 (fictional)

- From **March 3**, signing in to email, the intranet and payroll needs a password **and** a tap in the
  **Kestrel Verify** app on your phone.
- Set it up now: install Kestrel Verify, open **mykestrel.example/verify**, scan the code, tap **Approve** on
  the test request. It takes about **3 minutes**.
- Last year **61%** of the account takeovers in our industry started with a stolen password alone
  (Kestrel security review, 2027). A second step stops a stolen password from being enough.
- No phone? Ask the IT desk for a hardware key; they have **400** ready.
- If you get an Approve request you did not start, tap **Deny** and call the IT desk.

## Tone

Practical and reassuring. Nobody likes extra steps; show that it is one tap.
