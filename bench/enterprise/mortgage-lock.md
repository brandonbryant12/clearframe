# Brief: should I lock my rate?

**From:** Mortgage Advice, Fairhaven Private Wealth (fictional)
**For:** clients who have just been pre-approved for a mortgage; sent with the approval email
**Length:** 60–75 seconds, 16:9
**After watching they should:** understand what a rate lock does and book a call with their adviser to decide.

## Source: Fairhaven Mortgage Guide, rate locks (fictional terms)

- A **rate lock** holds your interest rate for a set period while your purchase closes: **45 days** as
  standard, **60** or **90** for a fee.
- If rates rise before closing, your locked rate stays. If they fall, a standard lock does not follow them
  down; a **float-down** option (one-time, 0.25% fee) lets you take a lower rate once.
- Example from the guide: on a **$400,000** loan over 30 years, a rate **0.5 points** higher costs about
  **$130 more a month**.
- Locks can be extended for **0.125%** per 15 days if closing slips.
- Your adviser can lock any business day; it takes one call.

## Tone

Clear and calm. Explain the trade-off, never predict rates, never push one choice.
