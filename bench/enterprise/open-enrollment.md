# Brief: open enrollment 2027, what changed

**From:** Benefits, Kestrel Mutual (fictional insurer, 4,200 employees)
**For:** all employees; most will watch the first 20 seconds and decide whether to keep watching
**Length:** about 75 seconds, 16:9
**After watching they should:** log in before **November 21** and check their medical plan and FSA.

## Source: Kestrel Mutual 2027 Benefits Guide, "What's new" page (fictional)

- Open enrollment runs **November 3 – November 21**. Choices take effect January 1.
- **New plan: Kestrel Saver**, a high-deductible plan with a health savings account (HSA). Kestrel puts
  **$750** (single) or **$1,500** (family) into your HSA in January. Deductible: $1,800 single / $3,600 family.
- **PPO premiums rise 4%**. For single coverage that is **$6 more per paycheck** (from $150 to $156,
  26 paychecks).
- **New: Calmline**, 12 free counselling sessions a year for you and each family member, no referral.
- Dental and vision: no changes.
- If you do nothing, you keep your current medical, dental and vision plans, but **your FSA election
  does not carry over**: it resets to $0. You must re-elect it.
- Where: myKestrel → Benefits → Enroll. Questions: benefits@kestrel.example.

## Tone

Clear and quick. People are busy and have seen this video every year. The one thing they must not miss
is the FSA reset.
