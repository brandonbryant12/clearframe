# Brief: more time at home

**From:** People Team, Alder Street Financial (fictional)
**For:** all employees, including people who are not parents yet; also used in recruiting
**Length:** 60 seconds, 16:9
**After watching they should:** know the new leave exists, who it covers, and where to apply.

## Source: Alder Street Family Leave Policy, effective January 1, 2028 (fictional)

- Paid parental leave rises from **8 weeks** to **16 weeks** at full pay.
- It covers **every** new parent: birth, adoption and foster placement, any gender.
- Eligible after **6 months** with Alder Street (was 12).
- It can be taken in up to **3 blocks** within the first year.
- Plus **4 weeks** of phased return at 80% hours on full pay.
- Apply in the People portal: Leave → Family leave → New request, at least **30 days** before the first block
  when you can.

## Tone

Warm and proud, but not sentimental. Concrete, not a values speech.
