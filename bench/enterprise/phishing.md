# Brief: three seconds before you click

**From:** Information Security, Alder Street Financial (fictional)
**For:** every employee; mandatory annual training, so the audience starts out bored
**Length:** 60 seconds, 16:9 and 9:16
**After watching they should:** use the **Report phish** button instead of clicking or deleting.

## Source: Alder Street Q2 2027 phishing simulation report (fictional internal results)

- In the Q2 simulation **14%** of employees clicked the link and **3%** entered their password.
- Only **22%** reported the email with the Report phish button.
- Three tells the report found in every simulated email that worked:
  1. **Urgency**: "your account will be locked in 2 hours".
  2. **A sender that is almost right**: alderstreet-secure.com instead of alderstreet.com.
  3. **A link or attachment you did not expect**.
- Reporting takes one click: the **Report phish** button in Outlook's toolbar. Security replies within
  an hour.
- Teams that reported more than half of simulated emails had zero real account takeovers in Q2.

## Tone

Quick, a bit of tension, never scary or patronising. Don't show real company logos.
