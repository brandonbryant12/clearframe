# Fixed-quantum allocation trays

`fframes/allocation-data.mjs` prepares a conserved integer token ledger. `fframes/allocations.mjs` draws it as native moving tiles and an exact before/after count chart. The [allocation studies kit](../examples/library-kits/allocation-studies/README.md) demonstrates I04 with original fictional household and work-hour plans in landscape and portrait. The separate B13 Blender mosaic remains unbuilt.

Call `allocationPlan(input)` with title, source, as-of date, period, qualification, quantum, buckets and an explicit ordered list of moves. The quantum declares `minorUnits` (a positive safe integer up to 1e9), `decimals` (0–6), `displayDecimals` (0–decimals) and a unit label. For example, `{minorUnits:10000, decimals:2, displayDecimals:0, unit:"USD"}` means one tile represents exactly 100 USD. Display precision must represent that quantum exactly; the formatter does not round it.

The pure model accepts two to six distinct buckets and two to 200 total tokens, including an empty bucket. Bucket IDs and displayed labels are unique. Counts are nonnegative integers. A move names two different existing bucket IDs as strings and a positive whole count available in its source. Up to 64 move groups may expand to at most 256 individual transfers. Sparse inputs, unknown fields, fractional tokens, overdraws and invented display precision fail before drawing. Inputs are not mutated.

Every token receives a stable ID, original bucket and slot. A move removes the last occupied source slot and fills the next free destination slot. This is a declared picture convention, not an optimizer. Ordered groups remain intact, including round trips. Ending counts, gross transfers and tokens whose final owner differs from their original owner are separate quantities. The model does not equate them. Exact integer minor-unit amounts, token IDs and rational count/total shares remain in the audit. Percentage labels rounded to one decimal use `≈`; displayed monetary or physical amounts remain exact.

`allocationClock(model, options)` exposes initial hold, individual move duration, intermediate settlement and final hold. Each option must be positive and no longer than 30 seconds; unknown options fail. Defaults are 3, 2.4, 0.6 and 7 seconds respectively. These are editorial durations, not measured transfer times. `allocationState(model, clock, time)` uses a half-open transit interval: debit the source at departure, credit the destination at arrival, and retain one explicitly unassigned token in transit in between. Parked plus in transit always equals the original total. Supply a validated model and its matching clock.

`allocationScene(input, {width,height,id,view,timing})` accepts `trays` or `comparison`, with optional timing passed to the clock. It returns props, model, geometry, clock and beat duration. The native layout supports two or three buckets, at most 36 tokens and eight individual moves; capacity and label guards may reject a denser arrangement. Supplied formats are 1920×1080 and 1080×1920. Landscape has a common lower lane; portrait uses gaps between stacked trays and an outer side lane. Every orthogonal route is checked against the complete stationary-token rectangles for the whole leg. A token translates only: size, rotation, opacity and identity stay constant.

Native amount and conservation readouts change at the exact departure/arrival boundaries. Token appearance does not create or destroy quantity. Equal tokens represent equal declared units, never equal risk, productivity, benefit or protection. The final comparison retains a common zero-to-total count scale, exact before/after counts, explicit ticks and approximate shares where needed. A zero amount has no filled bar, while its zero count remains labeled.

The fixtures model a reassigned plan only. There is no inflow, spending, loss, fee, return, optimization or changing token value. No actual financial or staffing recommendation is implied. One prototype reading-density warning per clip is retained: the heuristic accumulates distinct text from successive readout states. Independent source and sampled picture review record the visible-state counts and pacing limits. New copy, units, data density or timing require fresh review. Continuous subjective playback, square/4:5 and final master acceptance remain open.
