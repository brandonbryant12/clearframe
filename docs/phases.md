# Dated phase trails

`fframes/phase-data.mjs` validates paired x/y observations, and `fframes/phases.mjs` composes their dated trail as native canvas elements. This is the C15 cycle/phase concept expressed as a declared observation plot. It does not fit a cycle, predict a turn or infer causation.

Call `phaseTrail(input)` with a title, source, as-of date, x/y specifications, four quadrant labels, dated points, an existing `focusDate` and `smoothing: "none"`. Each axis declares a label including units, a prose definition, a finite increasing domain, three to six explicit ticks, an interior comparison reference and zero to six displayed decimals. Ticks include the domain endpoints and reference and must be exactly representable at the declared display precision. Linear positions preserve the supplied values; the helper never clips or independently normalizes data.

The model accepts two to 120 explicit dated rows. Dates are unique, increasing UTC calendar days and cannot exceed the as-of date. Each coordinate is a finite number within ±1e9 and its domain, or an explicit null. Missing array members, omitted coordinate fields, invalid dates and nonfinite values fail. One complete pair is required. Original coordinates, dates, row indices and missing reasons remain in the audit. Inputs are not mutated.

For a complete pair, compare x and y with their separately declared references. Strictly above/below both values selects `NE`, `NW`, `SW` or `SE`. Equality to x, y or both references is a separate boundary state, never forced into one of the four open quadrants. The four authored labels must remain distinct after displayed whitespace normalization. A missing pair has neither a quadrant nor a reference-boundary state. In the machine example, 60% load and 70°C are illustrative comparison references; they are not operating limits.

Segments connect only adjacent supplied rows when both pairs are complete. A null in either coordinate breaks both adjacent connections. Unlisted dates remain unobserved; the helper does not invent expected sampling intervals or insert values. The lower date strip and reveal times use the fraction of elapsed calendar days from the first supplied date to the last. Straight segments are explicitly labeled guides, not measured intermediate states. Observed markers appear at their actual coordinates; they never travel along the line or a smoothed curve. Coincident observations can share one plotted location while retaining distinct dates in the audit and strip.

`phaseScene(input, {width, height, id, view, reveal})` supports a complete trail or a fixed dated focus. Native composition is limited to 24 rows and frame dimensions from 640 to 4096; supplied examples use 1920×1080 and 1080×1920. The focus ring identifies the exact supplied pair and date, with explicit missing/boundary wording when relevant. Displayed rounded observation values receive `≈`; the audit keeps full precision. Axis tick labels are not approximate.

Quadrant copy occupies an estimated placement rectangle inside its own quadrant. Placement excludes every observed marker, selected-ring clearance and complete connecting segment; no value is displaced to make room. Dense dates, crowded axis text, insufficient quadrant space or a blocked copy zone fail before rendering. Such guards are engineering bounds, not a substitute for checking actual glyphs and encoded phone frames. New copy, data and aspect ratios need fresh review.

The four specimens have independent model, native, encoded and sampled visual reviews recorded in the kit. They remain prototypes. Continuous subjective playback and final master acceptance are separate gates. The current contract supports raw supplied pairs only; smoothing, inferred lead/lag, automatic cycle classification, fitted trajectories and causal conclusions are outside it.
