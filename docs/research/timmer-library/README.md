# A general motion library, tested against a year of macro explanations

**Research window:** October 2, 2025–October 2, 2026. **Prepared:** October 2, 2026.

Open the [searchable inventory](index.html). Its editable sources are [inventory.json](inventory.json) and [sources.json](sources.json). The [source register](SOURCES.md) records exactly what was reviewed and how it was accessed. The [package convention](../../library-packages.md) and [Balance study example](../../../examples/library-kits/balance-study/README.md) make one small part concrete now.

## Recommendation

Build a library of **explanatory operations**, backed by reliable chart primitives and a small collection of physical materials and camera rigs. Organize around what the viewer needs to understand: compare, separate drivers, expose a hidden relationship, follow a flow, reveal a constraint, change scale, or consider another outcome.

Timmer's material is a useful stress test because the recurring questions require very different pictures even when they concern the same market. The same strong index can conceal narrow participation, an earnings improvement can offset falling valuations, and an asset that diversified one period may move with the portfolio in another. These are demands on the visual system, not reasons to build a separate asset for every article.

This research delivers **71 inventory entries**: 18 charts, 12 explanatory compositions, 14 Blender operations, 10 camera/motion patterns, 8 supporting tools, and 9 retirement recipes. Some curate existing capabilities; others are proposed. Each entry has a stable ID, source links, parameters, existing implementation notes, dependencies, effort class, integrity constraints and nonfinancial uses. Priority is an editorial judgment about reuse and dependencies, not a measured frequency ranking.

## Evidence and limits

The corpus contains **24 dated records: 19 essays, editorial summaries or interview transcripts, plus 5 indirectly accessed X items**. It represents every month from October 2025 through September 2026 and includes the October 1, 2026 endpoint. It is a purposive sample, not an exhaustive list of his posts. Searches covered Fidelity's insight index, dated Fidelity articles/transcripts, Timmer-authored essays republished under Fidelity Canada's byline on AdvisorAnalyst, and indexed embeds of @TimmerFidelity posts.

The three publication forms are kept separate: an essay authored by Timmer; a publisher's summary of his commentary; and an interview transcript. X pages returned empty text or errors. Social records therefore link to the reporting page and, where resolved, the original post; they are discovery evidence, not independently authenticated transcripts. A blog and tweet repeating the same idea are not independent confirmation. Some sources were accessible only as indexed excerpts, explicitly noted in the register.

Dates are displayed publication dates, not necessarily the date of the underlying market observation. For example, S18's title refers to August 4 while its displayed date is August 5. No copied chart artwork, historical market dataset, Fidelity branding or Timmer voice is included in the kit. No investment forecast is endorsed. Future factual films will need usable source data and dates; this inventory needs the explanatory pattern, not his exact chart or conclusion.

Source prose is not automatically numerically sound. For example, S16 describes a correlation of minus 59% as almost perfect, which is not a characterization we should propagate; S15's wording about a percentage Z-score also needs clarification before reuse. The library should require explicit statistic definitions, units and source inputs rather than reproduce such wording.

## What exists and what is missing

The local audit used checkout `f00c6de` and the active CLI/library contracts. There are 33 native blocks, including single-series lines, bars, waffles, proportional forms, comparisons, equations and flows. Existing canvas tools include keyed motion, paths, cross-beat morphs, worlds, parallax, dollies, focus and chart transformations. The library also has mechanisms such as balance, cash-lock, pipeline, queue, scenario-fork, risk-lens and evidence-desk, plus eight retained Blender sculpture packages.

Those are strong ingredients. They are not yet a general statistical graphics suite. The `matrix` block is a comparison grid, not a correlation heatmap. The `cycle` block is a process diagram, not a quantitative phase-space plot. The single-series `line` contract is not a complete multiseries, forecast-vintage or logarithmic chart API. Arbitrary canvas paths make a one-off drawing possible, but do not supply reusable data transformations, scales, legends or integrity checks.

Blender already provides editable scenes, deterministic baked motion, prepared opaque clips and receipts. Generic camera rigs, tracked anchors, semantic action phases and transparent multipass delivery are proposals. The existing native renderer remains the final compositor. No replacement renderer, generalized plugin system or folder migration is needed.

## Build seven coherent collections

### 1. Drivers and counterforces

Pair a two-force native diagram with return decomposition, earnings revisions and a valuation sensitivity view. Use the existing brass mobile as a quiet introduction, then switch to flat evidence. Add a compressible frame only after the quantitative relationship works. Useful beyond finance for revenue, costs, engineering limits and policy tradeoffs.

**Assets:** C03, C04, C14, I01, B01, B05, M03. **Look:** restrained geometric typography, a controlled metal/ceramic object and clear flat evidence. **Variation:** one film starts from the total and separates it; another starts from a hidden opposing force; a third follows the changed forecast.

### 2. Many parts, one headline

Start close to a leader, pull back to a field, then distinguish equal participation from weighted impact. Carry stable identities into a tile field, ranking or regional comparison. A crowd with distributed light adds a physical reveal without turning a skyline into a misleading bar chart.

**Assets:** C05, C08, C18, I08, B08, M02. **Look:** printed dots or ceramic modules. **Variation:** spotlight a small group, reveal absent participation, or show participation spreading. Also useful for adoption, ecology and system health.

### 3. Flows, stores and constraints

Treat money, materials or work as things moving through a named system. Use flat reservoirs and routes for exact explanation, and a short gate/conveyor insert for a tactile transition. Separate the stock at a point in time from flows over an interval.

**Assets:** C16, I02, I05, I07, B04, B09, M05. **Look:** a bright technical workbench with colored tokens and readable connectors. **Variation:** follow one token, inspect a queue, or pull back to the reconciled balance. Also useful for logistics, payments and operations.

### 4. Time, expectation and changing regimes

Build a common time-series foundation, then add aligned histories, vintage estimates, cycle trails and scenario paths. An archival treatment can distinguish prior periods, but keep scales and dates explicit. Nested 3D rings are an optional horizon metaphor, not a forecast model.

**Assets:** C01, C02, C04, C10, C11, C12, C15, C17, B12, M08. **Variation:** compare two starts, inspect the revision, reveal a change in growth, or explain why an analogy stops working. Also useful for cohort analysis and scientific observation.

### 5. Relationships and diversification

Make the change in relationship the action: independent objects become coupled, or a fixed collection redistributes among trays. Then show the actual correlation window or allocation in native graphics. Do not let a pleasant balancing sculpture imply safety.

**Assets:** C07, C08, I04, I09, B06, B13, M04. **Look:** modular objects with contrasting motion rather than contrasting amounts of decoration. **Variation:** show coupling first, start from a failed expectation, or begin with the decision conditions. Also useful for team dependencies and sensor systems.

### 6. Investment and delayed payoff

Show construction before utilization, then distinguish adoption from measured productivity. Infrastructure can assemble into a lattice; a light reaches downstream objects only after a clear time gap. Native panels compare spending, capacity, usage and outcomes without forcing them onto incompatible scales.

**Assets:** C10, C18, I06, I07, B11, M01. **Look:** daylight structures, simple component silhouettes and restrained illumination. **Variation:** start with the unfinished network, follow a downstream beneficiary, or ask what evidence is missing. Also useful for education, energy and research investment.

### 7. Retirement, ownership and income

Follow contributions into an account, reveal the employer matching rule, distinguish ownership from access, and carry accumulation into withdrawals. Compare fees on identical assumptions. Build a pension formula separately from an account balance; show payout conditions without claiming actuarial equivalence.

**Assets:** R01–R09, C01, C09, C16, T02, I02, I07, I09, I10, I11, B04, B10, M10. **Look:** warm paper and ink for exact evidence, ceramic compartments and mechanical gates for selected physical explanations. **Variation:** start from a matching threshold, a departure date, a fee difference, a first payment or a reordered return sequence. Reuse the same operations for grants, project budgets and inventory.

This collection supplements the macro corpus; it is not attributed to Timmer. The [retirement source register](retirement-sources.json) records three primary references. Plan-specific inputs, service counting, payment quotes and formula adjustments must be supplied for a factual film. Sample fixtures are explicitly hypothetical.

## Camera and motion should be reusable assets too

Author camera patterns by **job**, with bounded parameters: inspect a detail, reveal the whole, follow a moving item, change attention, or move to an honest measurement plane. Start with native world/dolly/focus tools and only add Blender rigs where real geometry or lens behavior earns its cost.

Each future rig should retain subject bounds, start/end pose, lens or orthographic scale, focus target, action timing, settled hold, native handoff anchors and separate portrait framing. Bake the result. Provide a locked-camera alternative for every quantitative scene. Initial rigs should use keyframes and instancing; defer fluid, cloth and other simulation-heavy effects until a specific story justifies them.

A useful hybrid sequence is: **physical operation → stable end pose → matched native mark → exact evidence → wider context**. The proposed M06 handoff needs explicit anchors; the current engine cannot morph a baked MP4 object by native element ID.

## A bounded build order

The `Now` inventory group contains candidates for the first wave, not one oversized implementation task. Deliver in slices:

1. **Conventions and discovery — delivered here.** Package documentation, source register, searchable planning inventory and the Balance study kit. Source validation and retained-asset identity are checked. No new 3D asset render or finished specimen film is claimed.
2. **First quantitative foundation.** Start T01/T02 with C01 only: aligned dated series, fixed scale, gaps, units, provenance and a shared legend. Exercise it in a financial comparison and a nonfinancial operations comparison. Then add C02 and C04 using the same semantics.
3. **First mechanism pair.** Add C03/C16 and I02: an exact signed reconciliation and a qualitative flow explanation. Validate totals independently. Use existing B01/B02/B03 media rather than introducing another 3D rig at the same time.
4. **Participation and term structures.** Add C05 and C06. Check count versus weight, maturity spacing, date snapshots and vertical layouts. These provide more reusable value than another decorative asset collection.
5. **First new Blender operation.** Build B09, a bounded conveyor/gate recipe, with T05 phases and static T03 anchors. Provide a wide view, an inspection view and an independently composed portrait view. Preserve a readable settled hold and deterministic source replay.
6. **Broaden only after the proofs.** Add the correlation matrix, forecast bands, sensitivity views and generic Blender camera helpers. Keep alpha/multipass export, dynamic tracked labels and simulation-heavy assets in `Later` until a destination scene needs them.

Effort classes are relative: S is curation/narrow adaptation, M is a reusable composition or adapter, and L changes a quantitative contract, native capability or Blender rig. They are not time or cost estimates. Reserve at least the local 20 GiB minimum before heavy work and use `codex-heavy`, one Cargo job and at most two supported render/test workers. This task found about 19 GiB free, so it used existing media and lightweight source checks rather than launching a render.

## Keep repeated films visually distinct

For a new brief, choose the **argument** before the treatment. Write the narrative move, explanatory mechanism, evidence form, physical material, camera grammar and sound rhythm as separate choices. Compare against the last few films. Change at least the mechanism or story structure when the explanation changes; palette and seed variations are refinements only.

Three deliberately different treatments of one two-force question:

- **Mechanism:** a compressing frame introduces pressure; an exact decomposition shows what offsets it; a held comparison states the limit.
- **Investigation:** an annotated source makes a claim; revision lines reveal what changed; the missing assumption remains visible.
- **Spatial explanation:** follow a component through a system; pull back to its dependencies; resolve on a flat benchmark chart.

Avoid a fixed quota of Blender seconds. Some concepts need only excellent flat graphics. Others benefit from one short physical operation. Variety should come from explanation, camera intent and materials, not from adding motion to every mark.

## Definition of done for future assets

- **Native data:** an explicit input contract, a small independently computed fixture, fixed scales, meaningful missing-data behavior, visible units/source/as-of, and a truthful final hold. Demonstrate at least two different topics.
- **Blender:** original editable recipe and baked scene, deterministic seed, poster and clip, original receipt, action/hold phases, copy region and format status. Review actual motion, occlusion, loop restart or one-way settlement; hashes alone are not acceptance.
- **Hybrid:** exact handoff frame/pose, source labels that stay legible, and proof that a native copy revision leaves prepared media bytes unchanged.
- **Package:** clear tags, dependency and license records, a preview that does not autoplay, a reproducible adoption example and an honest lifecycle state.
