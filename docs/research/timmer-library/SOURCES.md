# Source register

Window: 2025-10-02 through 2026-10-02; researched 2026-10-02.

Representative, purposive survey across every month of the requested year, plus the October 1 endpoint. Not an exhaustive archive or a frequency estimate. Sources are deduplicated by page; blog/social overlap is not independent confirmation.

- Direct X access was unavailable or empty. Five social items are explicitly marked indirect evidence.
- Publisher summaries are distinguished from Timmer-authored essays and interviews. Dates on syndicated pages can differ from the underlying note date.
- Charts and market datasets were not licensed or imported. No market forecast or numerical conclusion is adopted as fact.
- Index-only search hits and out-of-window articles were excluded from the analyzed corpus.
