# A small set of Timmer-inspired style studies

User steering, October 2, 2026: include a few examples that draw visual styling from Timmer's social posts and published charts, including an interpretation for Blender graphics. Keep the current library direction and quality bar. This is a bounded companion to the asset build-out.

## Study set

1. **Native comparison chart:** inspect original chart images from two or three posts already in the source register. Record observed background, typography hierarchy, series colors, line weights, grid density, label placement and source treatment. Produce one editable comparison with original illustrative data. Judge which details survive motion and phone-size presentation.
2. **Annotated research graphic:** inspect the visual evidence for emphasis, callouts, highlighted time windows and comparison/inset arrangements. Adapt a small selection into a second native example whose emphasis helps explain an actual difference in the data. Retain honest scale and date labels.
3. **Blender companion:** translate the selected palette, contrast and visual hierarchy into a restrained material/lighting variant of a relevant mechanism, such as the balance or reservoir. Keep data, labels and source text in the native compositor. This is our three-dimensional interpretation; do not attribute a Blender style or animation practice to Timmer without evidence.

## Evidence and acceptance

The current corpus primarily records text/transcript research. It does **not** yet establish observed visual style. Before calling anything an extracted style, inspect actual chart imagery, link the exact post/image and date, and separate direct social access from publisher reproductions. Record observations separately from our adaptations. Use the existing Jurrien Timmer / @TimmerFidelity corpus as the reference set.

Retain a compact reference-to-design table and editable parameters, plus landscape/portrait and phone review. Keep quantitative geometry truthful when a source chart's visual density needs adaptation. Use original data and artwork for examples; do not bake source screenshots, branding or labels into reusable Blender media. An inspiration credit does not establish endorsement.

Start these studies after the current shared-scale chart readability and phase-label issues are resolved. Two native examples and one Blender interpretation are sufficient for this addition. They complement the seven collections; they do not replace the general theme system or restart the inventory.
