# Counterweight craft rejection — October 3, 2026

**Rejected for visual quality. Not a new library asset.** The user described the counterbalance as feeling cheap and low quality, then emphasized: “Details matter.” This feedback concerns the visual direction; it is not a request to change the mathematical truthfulness requirements.

The local framing experiment made the object larger and reserved native copy space. Its saved-scene check passed 145 baked poses, including rigid lengths, connections, full subject bounds, loop closure and arbitrary seeks. Those results did not make the picture good enough. The subsequent motion pass was stopped when the user rejected the direction. `interrupted-receipt.json` records SIGTERM; there is no completed motion result or approval for this candidate.

## What the picture gets wrong

- Thin rods, generic disc/block weights and the oversized base read as an assembly of default primitives.
- A largely empty studio, broad floor reflections and minimal material detail give it a generic demonstration-render appearance.
- Mechanical connections are mathematically coincident, but their modeled construction is unconvincing at close range: pivot hardware, suspension attachments, edge treatment and material transitions lack considered detail.
- The sculpture introduces “balance” without doing enough explanatory work for either proposed subject. More detail alone would not repair that weak purpose.
- Better bounds and a clean frame do not establish composition, taste, believable construction or meaningful motion.

## Consequence for the build

Pause expansion of this counterweight family. Preserve the historical balance-study package and its original source; do not promote it or the rejected framing experiment. Revisit an asset only with a specific explanatory purpose and a stronger designed picture. Do not treat extra bolts, finer samples or added surface noise as sufficient repair.

Before the next substantial motion render, inspect one complete art-directed still at intended display size and at a close detail crop. It must establish silhouette and proportion, deliberate negative space and type placement, coherent material scale, believable joins/contact, controlled highlights/shadows, and why this mechanism helps this explanation. Then inspect a short motion pass for weight, clearances, anticipation, action and settlement. Technical checks remain necessary evidence; they are not the visual acceptance gate.

This feedback applies to the library quality bar, not only this object. Existing assets retain their current evidence and prototype status. No completed package is retrospectively described as accepted.
