# The timing of cash

This is an invented teaching fixture, not a company report or investment recommendation.

## The question

A company can record activity before the payment becomes usable cash. What matters for its obligations is when that cash is available.

## One illustrative month

Opening cash is $60k. Collections add $40k and payments remove $70k. Closing cash is $30k [1].

| Cash movement | USD thousands |
| --- | ---: |
| Opening cash | 60 |
| Collections | 40 |
| Payments | 70 |
| Closing cash | 30 |

The arithmetic describes the month-end balance. It does not say whether the balance stayed positive on every day within the month. A daily timeline needs actual dates and settlement terms.

## Sources

[1] Authored illustrative ledger: 60 + 40 - 70 = 30. No historical company data.
