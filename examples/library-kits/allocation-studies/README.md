# Allocation trays

**Prototype.** Two original fictional plans move a fixed pool of equal-value tiles between native compartments, then compare the exact before/after allocation. Four retained landscape/portrait clips have zero native layout errors/warnings, zero detected pops and matching shuffled seeks. Continuous subjective playback and master acceptance remain pending.

- **Household plan:** twenty 100 USD tiles retain a 2,000 USD total. Two tiles leave Bills and two leave Flexible for a larger Buffer. Each 46-second clip has 22 seconds of transfers/holds and a 24-second comparison.
- **Work-hour plan:** twenty-four two-hour tiles retain 48 hours. The listed transfers include two tiles that leave Quality and return, preserving the difference between gross transfers and ending allocation. Each 58-second clip has 34 seconds of transfers/holds and a 24-second comparison.

The unit, unchanged total and fictional source stay visible. Counts debit on departure and credit on arrival; parked plus in transit always equals the total. Tokens never scale, duplicate or fade during a transfer. Final bars have the same zero-to-total count scale. Hours are equal units, not equal output; money allocation does not encode equal risk or protection.
