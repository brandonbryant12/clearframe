# Sources and provenance

All plans, bucket labels, amounts and transfer groups in `inputs.json` are original fictional fixtures. No actual household budget, company allocation, work schedule, market data or publisher image is reproduced.

The model is an original integer ledger: a fixed token quantum is stored as integer minor units, each departure removes one token from a source stack, and arrival adds that exact identity to a destination stack. There are no fractional tokens. Before, in-transit and after states retain the complete integer total. The explicit transfer order is authored input, not an inferred optimal allocation. Last-slot-first selection is a deterministic picture convention, not a financial or scheduling rule.

Exact counts and amounts require no external empirical claims. Percentage labels derive from count divided by the original total; approximate decimal displays are marked. Source lines visibly identify fictional allocation and the as-of date. Every storyboard retains the fixture and model reference, and `audit.json` keeps unrounded integer quantities, token identities, routes and timing.

Original implementation and assets are MIT-licensed. Native fonts retain their bundled SIL OFL notices and pinned provenance. No external art, code, dataset, voice or music is imported. B13 remains a separate unbuilt Blender proposal.
