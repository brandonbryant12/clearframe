# Bounded orbit and truck studies

Question: what becomes visible when only the point of view changes? Keep geometry still so the camera operation has explanatory purpose. This adds reusable T04 presets and separately composed landscape/vertical examples, not an automatic collision or visibility solver.

## Decisions

Continue the authorized library build-out with original procedural art, no paid generation or external models. Compare a flat native diagram, a continuously spinning sculpture and a bounded inspect-and-hold move. Choose the bounded move because depth, occlusion and parallax are the subject; it needs one deliberate change of view and an ending inspection hold.

The orbit studies an open ceramic assembly and exposes a side channel. Native subjects are a system-inspection metaphor and a research-method metaphor about checking another view. The truck moves camera and target together past foreground screens; native subjects are visibility in an inspection and incomplete reporting. None presents a measured system or a causal claim. Reusing geometry changes the editorial question; palette-only variants do not count as different subjects.

Warm paper, mineral ceramic, restrained graphite and blue/amber parts. No baked type, quantities, audio or decorative object motion. Native titles, phase labels and qualifications stay sharp in upper/lower bands. Eight seconds of prepared motion includes a held start and endpoint, followed by a ten-second explicit native final-frame hold. New copy must pass reading-rate critique; extend a true held pose instead of slowing the camera merely to fit words.

Orbit: fixed world-Z target, constant cylindrical radius, elevation and optics, one 68-degree arc. Truck: equal camera/target translation, constant orientation and optics, no pan or zoom substitute. Geometry and lights stay fixed. Independently fit each format; inspect all source frames for complete subject bounds and decode actual encoded phase/cut/end images at phone size. Check foreground/rear parallax against camera geometry and encoded movement where visible. Quantitative clearance does not establish arbitrary occlusion or collision safety.

Retain editable source, baked scenes, receipt hashes, phase clock, replay, deterministic seek evidence, independent review and limits. These are prototypes; sampled frames do not establish continuous subjective playback or final master acceptance. Other aspect ratios and arbitrary new scenes require new framing and review.
