# Sources and rights

The two scene recipes, analytic camera presets, explanatory copy and geometry are original ClearFrame work under the repository MIT license. No external models, data, textures, recordings or generated media are used.

`scripts/blender/camera_rig.py` implements circular rotation and equal translation directly. The independent checker derives orbit positions using polar coordinates and checks projected parallax against the inverse-depth relationship in a fixed perspective camera. These are geometric checks of authored scenes, not observed economic data or calibrated optics.

Blender is an optional offline preparation tool under its own license. Each retained receipt identifies the exact installed version, source hashes, settings and encoded output. Bundled native fonts keep their existing OFL notices. Native copy remains editable separately from prepared media.

The portrait truck deliberately changes bench width, screen spacing, route length and camera travel. These are illustrative arrangements; no physical equivalence or quantitative cross-format comparison is claimed.
