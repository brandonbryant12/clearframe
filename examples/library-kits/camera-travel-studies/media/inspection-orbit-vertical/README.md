# Inspect the other side

A bounded camera orbit around a static open assembly exposes another view of its side connection.

Use: Inspect hidden structure and distinguish a changing view from a changing object.

Keep native copy in the reserved upper and lower bands. The source camera is separately composed for landscape and portrait.

Original illustrative geometry; no measured capacity, amount, throughput or causal relationship.

Files: editable scene.blend (baked transforms; no auto-execution), poster.png, clip.mp4 and the PNG frame sequence, source scripts, render-config.json and receipt.json.

The receipt binds the exact source code, Blender version, settings and outputs. Inspect the clip, contact points and final settled hold before use.

To use in ClearFrame, copy clip.mp4 inside the film and register it as an asset with kind "clip". Place it with a video block or beat plate. Typography, figures, sources and narration remain native and editable.
