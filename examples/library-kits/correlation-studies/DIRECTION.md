# A relationship depends on the window and the paired sample

Question: do the same two fictional return streams move together in each declared window?

Tension: a single colored cell can hide missing observations, a changing sample and an undefined statistic. A smooth transition can imply measured intermediate coefficients that were never computed.

Turn: two explicitly dated Pearson matrices use the same member order and fixed signed color domain. Each cell includes its pair count. A constant member is undefined even on the diagonal. Too few paired months is a different stated reason. Select one outlined pair, then show the original monthly returns on synchronized native charts with identical percent axes and dated x domains. Hollow observations were not paired and did not enter that coefficient.

Payoff: the viewer sees what the coefficient summarizes and what it omits. No causality, prediction, significance or portfolio covariance inference is claimed. Pairwise matrices need not be positive semidefinite.

Three directions considered: a single changing scalar, a moving scatter plot, or a matrix followed by paired histories. Choose the matrix and histories to expose both the population of relationships and the actual dates behind a selected pair. The first matrix reveals whole cells with their values, without interpolating coefficients. The second is an explicit cut to a new six-month window. The third cuts to labeled original return histories; synchronized linear drawing follows calendar time, not ordinal point index. Missing observations disconnect adjoining segments. Stable holds are intentional reading time.

Use the paper palette and geometric title voice. Blue and orange distinguish positive and negative correlation, with explicit signs and a fixed -1/0/+1 legend; these colors do not mean good or bad. Pale fills retain dark numeric type. Grey and an em dash distinguish undefined cells from zero. Each window retains member order, selected-pair outline and visible method/minimum sample. Portrait uses stacked pair charts; landscape uses side-by-side charts. Text, scales and marks stay native. No 3D perspective or decorative camera movement changes a statistical picture.

The changing-pair case reverses one relationship and introduces a constant member. The missing-pairs case includes partial pairs and insufficient shared observations. Both are original supplied monthly simple-percent returns, not price levels or external market observations. No paid media, narration, synthetic voice or audio is used. These are silent reusable explanatory assets.

Decisions: implement and review the declared monthly Pearson contract first; reject unsupported frequencies/methods instead of guessing. Use explicit contiguous month-end grids, nulls, inclusive windows and at least three observations. The current examples require four. Retain exact unrounded coefficients and contributing dates in the audit. Review both formats, drawing fronts, cuts, undefined cells and paired/unpaired marks. Keep prototype status until applicable acceptance evidence exists; sampled review does not establish continuous subjective playback or a publication master.
