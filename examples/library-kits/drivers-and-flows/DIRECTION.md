# Drivers, histories and flows

Build C02, C03 and C16 as a related native kit. Each has two substantively different fictional cases, separately composed for landscape and vertical. Use the existing native compositor, explicit scales and source labels. A new generic renderer or a perspective diagram would not improve these comparisons.

## The explanatory work

- **Historical comparison:** make each observed anchor visible with its real date and original value, then put both records on elapsed days and index 100. The newer record stops at its actual supplied endpoint. An older-only band and native annotation distinguish the longer history; it is not a forecast. One case includes a missing older observation, which must break the path. Do not invent intermediate observations.
- **Product change:** expose factor units and start/end inputs, then separate the two baseline-weighted effects, their interaction and any independent additive amount. The share example holds cash without reinvestment and must not be described as a total-return index. The revenue example multiplies price by volume. Keep the interaction visible and reject negative/zero starting factors rather than implying a valid P/E interpretation where none exists. This is arithmetic attribution, not causal inference.
- **Stock reconciliation:** distinguish an opening stock, signed supplied changes and an independently observed close. Keep the unexplained remainder as a visibly separate component. Demonstrate both an asset balance and physical inventory. A positive residual is not automatically an inflow; a negative residual is not automatically an outflow or loss. Bridge row order is arithmetic, not transaction timing.

## Picture and motion

Use warm paper, blue/orange signed contributions, dark opening/closing totals and a muted violet unexplained component. Geometric native type, explicit units, sparse scale guides and a separate numeric column should support phone reading. Portrait gets its own geometry, not a crop.

Horizontal contribution rows keep equal rectangle thickness. Every rectangle length and area must be proportional to its absolute amount on the declared shared domain. Opening/closing rectangles start at zero; floating changes start at the prior cumulative level. Zero quantities have no filled rectangle. Use opacity and staged arrival only: never move a quantitative endpoint to create an entrance. Keep camera and scales fixed. Inspect native geometry and actual encoded edges during the fade as well as the hold.

History paths use the shared elapsed-day reveal clock. No future current-series segment exists. Final values arrive after settlement. Formula beats reveal inputs before results and reserve a useful reading hold. Silent holds are intentional; do not add idle motion to hide diagnostics.

## Review

Keep original inputs, method version, input hash, calculation outputs, source files, receipts, contact/phone sheets and encoded checks. An independent reviewer must inspect arithmetic, missingness, units, phase identity, density and source legibility. Correct substantive defects before committing the milestone. Source or sampled encoded checks alone do not promote these to accepted finished-film assets; continuous playback remains a separately recorded requirement.
