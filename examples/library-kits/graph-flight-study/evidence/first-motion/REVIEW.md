# First graph-plane motion test

Exploratory 6.5-second, 156-frame, 960x540 H.264 draft. Parent and independent reviewer inspected sampled decoded frames. The upright 2D chart and intended weave are recognizable; the ending shows all eight bars on one baseline. This is suitable for user feedback, not asset acceptance.

Findings: around 4–4.5 seconds, close blue faces obscure most of the chart; recover the baseline and neighboring bars sooner. The overview appears by 5 seconds; continuous playback must assess whether the pullout is rushed or abrupt. Grid lines compete during close passes; soften their contrast. No subjective smoothness or acceleration claim is made from sampled frames.

Native labels/date/value readouts, two distinct data cases, independently composed portrait, a reusable validated camera API, encoded data/pose verification and continuous playback remain open. The original fictional values are [18,25,22,34,42,38,53,61], with height .085 world units per value. The staged height reveal is an animation, not intermediate observed data.
