# Participation and benchmarks

C05 and C08 extend the existing native chart foundation. Use original fictional inputs and existing native rectangles, circles, lines and type. Four cases demonstrate different questions, each in separately composed landscape and vertical. The adapter preserves full dated membership; the signed branch compares a metric with an explicit benchmark and gives area a separate meaning.

## Questions and structure

C05 first asks how many of twelve members meet a stated condition. Every tile has equal area and a stable member label. Condition colors are categories, not a magnitude ramp. The next scene uses the same full membership but replaces count with a declared total weight; the three shares retain missing observations. The market case has two positive-return members carrying 65% of opening weight; the service case has six qualifying services and two missing uptime records. Counts, weights and membership dates must stay visible. Neither scene claims a weighted mean or changes weights in response to later returns.

C08 first names the original metric, benchmark, period and separate size measure. Then four branches use a fixed zero-centered, explicitly ticked percentage-point axis. Blue means above the benchmark, orange below it; these colors do not claim that either direction is good. Filled circle area, not radius, is proportional to a nonnegative amount. The two cases use fictional price returns/sample holdings and completion rates/end-period open cases. A true zero size has no filled circle: a small native cross locates its observed endpoint. A missing metric has no quantitative endpoint, even if its size is known. Differences are not ratios, causal effects or return indices.

## Geometry and motion

Use paper, geometric type and the existing blue/orange palette, with violet for missing observations. Reserve a readable native source block. Equal tiles are countable; weighted segments share one height. Circle radii follow the square root of size on one fixed scale. Stems, endpoints, tile areas, segment lengths and circle areas never animate away from their data values. Entrance opacity and staged reading are the only quantitative motion. Fixed cameras preserve direct comparison. Native numeric labels explain small/zero marks without enlarging them.

Review both sides of each cut, early data arrivals, settled marks and final reading holds at 360 pixels. Inspect actual encoded tiles/segments and circle centers/radii across eligible frames, including visible fades. Retain inputs, input hash, source calculations, native jobs/receipts, phone sheets, seek hashes and independent critique. The kit remains a prototype until an observed continuous playback pass and final quality review are recorded.
