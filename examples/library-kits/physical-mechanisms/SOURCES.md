# Sources and boundaries

The recipe geometry, motion, case labels and input fixtures are original work. There is no imported model, texture, chart image or market dataset. Inventory [B04 and B09](../../../docs/research/timmer-library/inventory.json) provide the asset brief; these examples do not reproduce a source video or claim new source-derived styling. The research-paper palette and material family continue the previously documented research-study kit.

The two reservoir subjects are illustrative resource access and tank operation. They share one geometric model. Its fill volumes are internally conserved, but their levels are not money, observed quantities, pressure, or a predicted flow rate. The two conveyor subjects are warehouse routing and a network routing metaphor. Four visible packets are graphical actors; their spacing and velocity are not samples of a measured queue or throughput model.

Implementation references:

- [Blender camera projection API](https://docs.blender.org/api/5.2/bpy_extras.object_utils.html): normalized camera coordinates used to bound the subject and reserve native copy regions.
- [Blender material API](https://docs.blender.org/api/4.2/bpy.types.Material.html): material surface render methods. The panels use alpha blending for a stylized clear view, without a refractive optical-glass or fluid simulation.
- [EEVEE material changes](https://developer.blender.org/docs/release_notes/4.2/eevee/): surface rendering context. The installed Blender version and exact retained source hashes are recorded in each render receipt.

The geometry checker measures baked transform/dimension invariants and projected bounding boxes. It does not prove real mechanics, fluid dynamics, network behavior, artistic quality, rendered occlusion or continuous-playback smoothness. Native video frame sampling converts 24 fps prepared motion into a 30 fps composition; no intermediate physical simulation is inferred.

Original files use the repository MIT license. Native bundled fonts keep their existing OFL notices. No paid provider call was made.
