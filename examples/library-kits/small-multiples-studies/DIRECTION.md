# Change the order, keep the comparison

Question: which comparable series is highest or lowest on one declared date, and what history sits behind that rank?

Tension: a reordered dashboard can silently change dates, omit missing members or rescale its selected panel. That makes order or apparent steepness look like new evidence.

Turn: retain each panel's full dated record on identical axes. Only the declared ranking date changes. Ties share a competition rank; missing values stay unranked at the end. Then enlarge one named panel uniformly while preserving both domains.

Payoff: the viewer can separate ranking, trajectory and attention. Selection need not mean the best or largest member. Missing ranking values are not zero or stale carried-forward observations.

Three directions considered: an overlaid multiseries chart; separate ranked bars that hide history; or equal-size native panels followed by one uniform focus expansion. Choose panels because the operation compares the history behind the order. Dates and domains remain visible, text stays native, and no perspective is introduced.

Use a paper palette, geometric title voice, blue observations and warm orange focus. Four panels have an explicit equal plotting area. Portrait has separately sized, taller panels; landscape has wider panels. Abbreviate month labels only when the declared full date range lies within one year. Preserve nonuniform calendar spacing and disconnected null gaps. No data value interpolates during a rank change or expansion.

The first grid reveals all series on the same elapsed-date clock. The second grid is a deliberate cut to a newly labelled ranking snapshot of the SAME full history, not a forecast vintage. Persistent series names and IDs preserve identity. No animated rank interpolation is implied. The final grid dims nonselected panels in .35 seconds, then enlarges the selected panel from .6 to 2 seconds. Its axes, labels and marks all undergo the same uniform transform. The domains remain identical. Long stable holds are intentional reading time, without decorative movement.

Two original fictional cases: delivery duration ranks ascending, with a missing June member and two equal leaders; household net flows rank descending, with a signed common scale and a selected negative member. Net flows are not balances or investment returns. Demonstrate and review landscape and vertical independently. No audio, paid generation or external dataset is used. These are silent reusable assets, not narration or captioned finished films.

Before packaging, inspect native sheets and actual encoded phone frames during the reveal, rank cut, fade, expansion and settled hold. Independently verify ranks, retained date/scale identity, missing gaps and encoded affine mapping at intermediate expansion frames. Keep the lifecycle prototype until applicable acceptance evidence exists; bounded samples do not establish continuous subjective playback or a final master.
