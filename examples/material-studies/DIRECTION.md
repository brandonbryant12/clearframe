# Material study direction

Purpose: compare reusable native forms, not tell a narrative. Preserve a consistent native label layout so changes in geometry, shading and motion are easy to judge.

Audience: directors selecting a library element for light professional, playful or dark brand films.

The first imagegen board explored paper, optical glass and inflated rubber. The second simplified topology. Rebuild selected ideas in native geometry and palette tokens; never use generated text or promise exact generated brand colours. See `docs/design/material-studies/prompts.json` for the reference prompts and `docs/image-direction.md` for production decisions.

Review checkpoints completed: baseline library review; two generated boards inspected; native landscape sheets independently reviewed; glass bead, paper edges, ribbon density, ring overlap and label spacing revised; portrait sheets and decoded timelines inspected. QA held findings remain advisory: these intentionally slow material loops have visible frame-zero art and no delayed information cue. Silent previews do not assess speech or music. Real-time motion review is still useful before choosing an asset for a final client film.

`make.mjs` regenerates this project plus `material-play` and `material-arena`. The professional family keeps small, slow ambient movements and a gentle `art.drift` of 0.5; the playful and arena families have stronger float, rotation and rock, and drift 0.8. Each beat has its own seed. The daylight study still measures as held (0.77 change per second): its pale, low-contrast push is deliberate and reviewed in motion. Every displayed word uses a bundled native font role. All colours in the art are palette tokens or derived gradients, not hard-coded image colours. No sourced factual claims appear.
