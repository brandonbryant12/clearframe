# Native status

The production path is the root CLI, `film/` and the renderer in `../scene/native`. The 33-block library, native stages, palette controls, motion vocabulary and speech timing contract are active. See `../docs/scene-engine.md` for the renderer and `../docs/verification.md` for validation evidence. `../docs/fframes-migration.md` is the historical record of the move from the browser renderer to FFFrames; FFFrames itself is now retired (`../archive/fframes/`).

The original two-scene comparison is historical and preserved under `../archive/comparison/`. Its speed measurements are specific to the frozen fixture; they are not a universal performance guarantee for the expanded library.
