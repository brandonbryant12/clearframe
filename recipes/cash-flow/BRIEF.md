# Follow the money until it becomes usable cash

Audience: Business owners, finance teams and customers learning a cash or settlement mechanism

Inputs: An approved description of the mechanism, cash dates and amounts on one basis, opening balance, constraints and brand assets

Arc: a payment approaches a gate, the timing question lands, comparable flows expose the gap, the balance changes, a short breath, then the decision. Wide mechanism -> close statement -> evidence -> numerical insert -> pause -> button. The cash-lock sketch is qualitative, not scaled to the example values or a promise about clearing times. The sample closes at $30k because $60k + $40k - $70k = $30k. Replace the complete example together. See docs/financial-playbooks.md for source and adaptation guidance.
