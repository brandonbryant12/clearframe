Inputs: The quarterly letter: returns as of the quarter end, the sharpest move and its recovery, the target mix, the committee's decision, how to book a review

Client arc: the quarter as one calm line (the drop and the recovery), the mix that held, what the committee did, one sentence of outlook in the committee's words, and the way to talk to an adviser done in the app. No predictions.
