# A documentary cold open: a detail, the place, a voice, the scale, then the title

Audience: Viewers of a true story, a case study or reportage

Inputs: One vivid detail with a time and place, a first-hand quote, one sourced figure, a title

Start in the middle of the story, not with its title. The quote and the figure are illustrative placeholders: replace them with real ones and their sources before publishing.
