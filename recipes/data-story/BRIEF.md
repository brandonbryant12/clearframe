# Tell a story with a few trustworthy numbers

Audience: A general audience meeting the numbers for the first time

Inputs: Three to five sourced figures, their dates and denominators

One dataset carries the film, in one object: a request card. The card that asks the question carries the average wait (4.2 days ≈ 100 hours), then becomes the first of a hundred cards, one per hour; the edit cuts in to each stage (52 + 30 + 12 + 6), then to the spread and its tail, and ends on the hundred cards with the waiting lit. Shots run three to four seconds and cut on what changes in the picture. Replace the figures and keep them consistent; draw the thing your numbers count (library/playbooks/_data-story.mjs); end on it, not on a card of text.
