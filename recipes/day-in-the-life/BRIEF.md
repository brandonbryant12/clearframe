# Follow one person through a benefit

Audience: Employees who see themselves in someone like them: new hires, parents, people near retirement

Inputs: One realistic person (a role, not a real employee), the moments the benefit touches in their week, the figures from the plan document and the step they take

Persona arc: meet one person (a cast `person` with their name), follow one object of theirs (here the paycheck) through the moments the benefit touches, and end with them, changed. The person is a role, never a real employee. Replace the name, the objects, sketchText, sketchParams and sketchSay with your plan's words and figures (`clearframe sketch NAME`).
