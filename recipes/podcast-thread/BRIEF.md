# Follow one podcast idea as a spacious visual essay

Audience: Listeners who want to stay with one thought rather than scan a wall of captions

Inputs: An exact recording excerpt and measured word timestamps, one tension, a concrete example and a reframe already present in the excerpt

Use the actual recording as the timeline: clearframe ingest --audio episode.wav --words words.json --from START --to END. Select an excerpt whose own words contain a tension, an example and a reframe. Keep its wording, speaker identity, order, measured timestamps and meaningful pauses intact. This playbook's narration is only an authored demonstration; never put it in a real speaker's mouth. The thread is a qualitative metaphor, and the small meter uses actual narration audio. Adapt the drawings to the idea in the recording, with one recurring object, deliberate negative space and a camera move at the change of thought. Keep only essential phrases as type. A silent hold belongs only where the recording contains one or the edit deliberately leaves room after the excerpt. Pair with the calm treatment. See docs/source-playbooks.md.
