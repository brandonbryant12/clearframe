# Explain a policy change and what to do now

Audience: Staff who skim policy documents and get caught by the change

Inputs: The policy before and after, the one rule people break most (with your own number), the deadline and where to act

Policy arc: the cost of getting it wrong as your own number, the document people must get right with its parts marked, the new deadline on a calendar, the step done in the app, the line to repeat spoken over the finished step. Replace sketchText, sketchParams and sketchSay with the policy's words and figures (`clearframe sketch NAME`). See skills/clearframe-workplace/SKILL.md.
