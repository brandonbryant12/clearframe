# Follow a few things through a process

Audience: People learning how a workflow, product or team handles its pieces: new hires, customers, stakeholders

Inputs: The real things being handled (requests, files, orders, reports, tickets) and what happens to them, step by step

A cast keeps the same objects on screen through every cast scene, so the film is moves of one picture rather than a card per line (docs/cast.md). Replace the objects with the real things in your process (choose icons from `clearframe catalog canvas`) and each formation with what actually happens to them, cued with `say` to the word that names the move. Keep the cast for the setup; cut to a chart, stage or card only at the turn or for evidence, and give every number its own sourced form. End on the picture (the line playing) rather than a card. Check both landscape and vertical.
