# Turn a long report into a question-led short film

Audience: Busy readers who will not open the report

Inputs: An evidence brief (clearframe ingest --markdown): the question, 3–5 sourced claims, one tension

Start from clearframe ingest --markdown report.md: it writes BRIEF.md with every figure, its sentence and its source, plus tensions and chart-ready tables. Replace every sample claim; lead with the question and the surprise, not the report's section order. Shots, not slides: the figure as a full-frame insert, the question as poster type, charts under lower thirds with a moving camera, a silence before the turn.
