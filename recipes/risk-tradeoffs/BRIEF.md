# Inspect the opportunity and the risk in the same frame

Audience: Financial educators and customers comparing the terms of a financial product

Inputs: Approved benefit and risk statements, current product terms, fees and access conditions, the audience's question and authorised brand assets

Arc: the attractive headline opens, a moving lens inspects access, price and loss, opportunity and limitation share equal screen space, silence separates the review from the decision, then the viewer gets a concrete question. Keep benefits and material risks equally prominent in narration, type size and hold time. The lens graphic is qualitative and does not rank products or measure risk. Do not add return figures without their period, fees, assumptions and relevant risk context. See docs/financial-playbooks.md.
