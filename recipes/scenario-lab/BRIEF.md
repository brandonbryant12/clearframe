# Test a financial decision against several possible futures

Audience: Planning teams and customers who need to understand assumptions and uncertainty

Inputs: The decision, a shared starting point, explicitly defined scenarios on the same basis, assumptions, decision triggers and approved brand assets

Arc: one number hides uncertainty, a close view establishes one origin, a pullback in the same world reveals the possible paths, a common-scale chart compares the sample, the limitation gets its own spoken scene, a breath, and an explicit decision trigger closes. Paths and equal-size markers do not encode probabilities. Scenario amounts use the same period and units. These illustrative inputs are not a prediction, confidence interval or recommendation. Replace the branches, assumptions, figures and sources as one dataset. See docs/financial-playbooks.md.
