# A trailer: a cold open, a rising montage, a silence, the title and a button

Audience: An audience to build anticipation in: a launch, a season, an announcement

Inputs: One promise, three images of what it touches, a title, a date or call to action

Shots, not slides (docs/cinema.md). Every world here belongs to this film's promise (a signal): a waveform out of the dark, power lines at dusk, a city switching on, a mast at dawn. Swap them for the places your promise touches, keep the rhythm: each montage shot shorter than the last, a black card between them, a silence with no voice, the title on the one flash, a button. Cards take a small LEAD line and one or two huge words (sketchText LEAD and CARD).
