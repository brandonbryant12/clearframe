# A trailer: a cold open, a rising montage, a silence, the title and a button

Audience: An audience to build anticipation in: a launch, a season, an announcement

Inputs: One promise, three images of what it touches, a title, a date or call to action

Shots, not slides (docs/cinema.md). Retime each sketch's dolly and focus to its line; each montage shot is shorter than the last. The silence beat has no voice on purpose, and the title lands after it.
