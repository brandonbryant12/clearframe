# Train one habit: the threat, its signs, the right move

Audience: Everyone in a mandatory training, bored before it starts

Inputs: Your own results (a simulation, an audit, incidents), the signs that gave it away and the one action people should take

Training arc: your own number as the hook, the message itself with its signs marked one by one, the habit done in the tool people use, the line to repeat spoken over it. Replace sketchText, sketchParams and sketchSay with your material (`clearframe sketch NAME`). For a policy or a receipt rather than an email, inspect marks other parts (sender, subject, body, link, attachment). See skills/clearframe-workplace/SKILL.md.
